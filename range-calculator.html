How the estimate works

Range is battery energy (watt-hours) divided by how much energy each mile takes. We start from typical fat-tire e-bike consumption - about 12 Wh per mile at low assist, 18 at medium, 25 at high and 30 on the throttle - then adjust:

  • Total weight (you, cargo and the bike): every 50 lb above 250 adds about 10%.
  • Terrain: rolling hills +20%, gravel +25%, steep hills +45%, sand or snow +70%.
  • Speed: raising the limit from 20 to 28 mph adds 8-25%, because air resistance climbs steeply with speed.
  • Cold: lithium batteries deliver less in the cold. We count 90% of the battery as usable in warm weather, down to 60% below freezing.

It's an estimate. Wind, tire pressure (Kingbull recommends 20-28 PSI depending on the model), stops and starts and battery age all move it. Plan long rides with a margin, or carry a spare battery.
