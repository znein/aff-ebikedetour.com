Which pair makes sense

  • Cheapest way to get two bikes: two Rangers. Both get the 18Ah battery.
  • Different heights: the Ranger fits riders from 5'0" and the Hunter 2.0S up to 6'6". Check each rider against the fit guide before mixing models.
  • One rider wants step-through: the Discover ST 2.0 pairs with either a Ranger or a Hunter 2.0S.
  • One bike has to fold: Hunter 2.0S + Literider 2.0.
  • Shared chargers: the Ranger and Hunter 2.0S use the same 18Ah battery and 2A charger, so a two-Ranger, two-Hunter 2.0S or Ranger + Hunter 2.0S household can share spares. Battery fit.
