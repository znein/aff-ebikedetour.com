Short riders (under 5'3")

The Ranger (from 5'0"), Expedition (5'1"), and the 20-inch Literider 2.0, Verve and Voyager 2.0 (5'2") fit best. The Discover 2.0, Rover 2.0 and EX Titan start at 5'5"-5'6".

Tall riders (over 6'4")

The EX Titan, Rover 2.0 and Jumper Go go to 6'7". The Ranger tops out at 6'3".

Heavier riders and cargo

450 lb payload: Trekker, Verve, Jumper Go, EX Titan, Voyager 2.0 and Dual Battery Voyager 2.0. The Ranger and Expedition are rated to 350 lb. Extra weight also cuts range: try the range calculator.

20-inch vs 26-inch wheels

All of these run 4-inch-wide fat tires. 20-inch wheels make a lower, shorter, more maneuverable bike that's easier to get on and to store - and they're what the folders and cargo bikes use. 26-inch wheels roll over ruts, roots and potholes more easily and hold speed better, which is why the mountain bikes (Hunter, Rover, EX Titan) use them.

Folding

The Literider 2.0 and Verve fold to 30.7 × 17.7 × 31.5 in. They still weigh 71-77 lb, so folding helps with space more than with lifting.
