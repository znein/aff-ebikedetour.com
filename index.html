Questions people ask

Where do Kingbull bikes ship from, and is shipping free?

Free shipping to the lower 48 US states (no PO boxes or APO); $230 per bike to Hawaii. Bikes ship from California, usually within 2 business days, and take 2-7 days by UPS/FedEx. Prices include tax in the 48 states.

What's the return policy?

15-day returns, approved by email first, in the original packaging. Cancelling after an order ships costs a 10% processing fee plus $150 return shipping; cancelling before it ships (after the first 2 hours) costs 8%.

What warranty do they have?

2-year warranty on new bikes; open-box bikes get 1 year.

How far will one actually go on a charge?

Kingbull's range numbers are for ideal conditions (light rider, flat ground, low assist). Heavier riders, hills, sand, high assist, throttle use and cold weather all cut range - often to half the rating or less. Our range calculator gives an estimate for your conditions.

How fast do they go?

Most top out at 28 mph with pedal assist and 20 mph on the throttle alone. Several (Discover 2.0, Discover ST 2.0, Literider 2.0, Trekker, Verve) ship set to 20 mph and can be adjusted. See e-bike classes & speed for what that means legally.

Which is best for a tall or heavy rider?

The EX Titan, Rover 2.0 and Jumper Go fit riders up to 6'7". For payload, the Trekker, Verve, Jumper Go, EX Titan and both Voyagers are rated to 450 lb. The fit guide lists every bike.

Is there an age limit?

Kingbull doesn't sell e-bikes to anyone under 18.
