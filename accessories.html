What to buy first

  1. Helmet: these bikes reach 28 mph.
  2. Lock (and maybe an alarm): a $1,000+ bike is a target.
  3. Pump and sealant: fat tires are sensitive to pressure, and an 80 lb bike is miserable to push home.
  4. Carrying: a rack, baskets or bags, depending on your bike - check the fit guide first.
  5. Range: a spare battery, or the power station if you camp.
