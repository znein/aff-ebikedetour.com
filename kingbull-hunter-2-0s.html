Good fit if…

  • You want a full-size 26" mountain-style fat-tire bike under $1,000.
  • You want range: 864Wh (18Ah) and an 80-mile ideal-conditions rating.

Think twice if…

  • Front suspension only - for rough, rooty trails look at the Rover 2.0 or EX Titan.
  • Cadence sensor rather than torque sensor.
  • It uses its own 2A charger; the 3A Kingbull charger isn't compatible.
