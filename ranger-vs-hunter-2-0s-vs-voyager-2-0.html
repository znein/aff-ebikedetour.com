Pick the Ranger if…

You want the lowest price and the moped look. At $749 it has the same 18Ah battery as the Hunter 2.0S, and it's the only one of the three with rear shocks. It also fits the shortest riders (from 5'0"). The costs: a steel frame that makes it the heaviest (88 lb), the lowest payload (350 lb), and no Kingbull universal rack.

Pick the Hunter 2.0S if…

You want a real 26-inch mountain-style bike for trails and you're 5'3" or taller. Big wheels roll over ruts and roots more easily than 20-inch ones. Same battery as the Ranger, 11 lb lighter, 400 lb payload. It has no rear suspension, though.

Pick the Voyager 2.0 if…

You'll carry things. It's the only one of the three with a torque sensor (smoother, more natural assist), a 450 lb payload and a 180 lb rear rack, and you can add an external battery later. Its 720Wh battery is the smallest of the three, so if range matters from day one, look at the Dual Battery Voyager. Kingbull says it is not designed for carrying children or pets.
