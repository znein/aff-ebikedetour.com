What the table tells you

  • Battery size is the biggest difference. Four sizes: 720Wh (Hunter 2.0, Literider 2.0, Discover, Voyager, Rover), 864Wh (Ranger, Hunter 2.0S), 960Wh (Trekker, Verve, Jumper Go, EX Titan) and the big two - Dual Battery Voyager (1,440Wh) and Expedition (1,512Wh).
  • Torque sensor vs cadence sensor changes how the bike feels more than any number does. Discover 2.0, Trekker, Verve, Rover 2.0 and others use torque sensors; the Ranger, both Hunters, Literider and Expedition use cadence sensors. Which you want.
  • Rider height ranges really differ: 5'0" minimum on the Ranger up to 6'7" maximum on the EX Titan, Rover 2.0 and Jumper Go.
  • Weight: 70 lb (Hunter 2.0) to 94 lb (Expedition). If you'll lift it onto a car rack, it matters.
