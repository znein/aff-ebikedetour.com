Good fit if…

  • You want the most bike for the least money: $749 buys the same 864Wh battery as the $949 Hunter 2.0S.
  • You like the retro moped look: long padded seat, round headlight, turn signals and a horn.
  • You're on the shorter side: the 5'0" minimum rider height is the lowest Kingbull lists.

Think twice if…

  • At 88 lb it's one of the heaviest bikes here (steel frame), so it's hard to lift onto a car rack.
  • 350 lb max payload is lower than most of the range (400-450 lb).
  • Cadence sensor: the motor responds to whether you pedal, not how hard - fine for cruising, less natural on climbs.
  • Kingbull's universal rear rack doesn't fit it (it has its own rear rack built in).

Before you buy

Shipping: Free shipping to the lower 48 US states (no PO boxes or APO); $230 per bike to Hawaii. Bikes ship from California, usually within 2 business days, and take 2-7 days by UPS/FedEx. Prices include tax in the 48 states.

Returns: 15-day returns, approved by email first, in the original packaging. Cancelling after an order ships costs a 10% processing fee plus $150 return shipping; cancelling before it ships (after the first 2 hours) costs 8%.

Warranty: 2-year warranty on new bikes; open-box bikes get 1 year.

Power station option: +$259 for the Nextcore640 (it's $299 on its own).

Buying two? Two Rangers is $1,348 (save $150); Ranger + Voyager 2.0 is $1,748 (save $200); Ranger + Discover ST 2.0 is $1,748 (save $200). All bundles.
