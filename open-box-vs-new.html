What you give up with open box

  • Warranty: 1 year instead of 2.
  • Returns: all sales final - no 15-day return.
  • Condition: Grade A is factory sealed, Grade B opened but unused, Grade C lightly test ridden. Shipping scratches aren't covered.
  • Colors may differ from the photos because of generational updates.

When it's worth it

The best open-box deal when we checked was a Grade B Discover 2.0 at $889, $310 under the new price. The Rover 2.0 Grade B ($1,129, $170 off) was also a real saving. For the Ranger, Hunter 2.0S and Trekker, new was the same or better value - you keep the 2-year warranty and the return window.

Rule of thumb: check the new price on the same day. If open box isn't at least $100-150 cheaper, buy new.
